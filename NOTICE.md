# Publication and Evidence Notice

LifeTwin is an independent competition and research prototype. It is not an
official Hithium product, does not contain Hithium internal data, and must not be
interpreted as a product-performance statement or a validated 15-25 year
forecast for utility-scale cells.

## Included

- Original LifeTwin source code for the research prototype.
- A normalized condition-mean table derived from the Naumann calendar-aging
  dataset, which is published under CC BY 4.0.
- Aggregate, derived experiment summaries and figures.
- Research notes, validation boundaries, and reproducibility tests.

## Deliberately Excluded

- Raw FastCharge, CellJAR, or other large upstream archives.
- Cell-level Stanford Lam/Joule samples, author code, or email correspondence;
  their data/code licensing was still being clarified at release time.
- Noncanonical experiments, local temporary files, and repeated artifact copies.
- Proprietary, station, or manufacturer-internal measurements.

The numerical results in this repository are retrospective development results
on public data. Independent long-duration LFP data and target-product data are
required before any deployment or commercial accuracy claim.
