## 发送步骤

1. 在 Battery Archive 原回复邮件中点击“回复全部”。
2. 保留原邮件主题和历史内容，便于对方确认其已经提供的数据。
3. 粘贴下面正文，以 `Jincheng Liu` 署名发送。
4. 收到回复后，将完整邮件保存为 `.eml` 或打印为 PDF；不要只保留截图。
5. 在获得明确同意前，不向 GitHub 上传原始数据、RPT 派生轨迹、单电芯结果或汇总结果。

## 邮件正文

Subject: Clarification on competition use and aggregate reporting of the SNL LFP data

Dear Battery Archive Team,

Thank you again for sharing the SNL LFP files and metadata. We have preserved the
source attribution and the citation to Preger et al. (2020), DOI:
10.1149/1945-7111/abae37. The data are currently used only for local,
non-commercial research and model development.

Before we use the work in a non-commercial energy-storage AI competition or
publish any project material, could you please confirm whether the following
scope is permitted?

1. Model training and evaluation for a non-commercial research competition.
2. A public GitHub repository containing our original code, frozen protocols,
   and data-loader instructions, but no SNL raw files or row-level derived data.
3. Competition reports or presentations containing only aggregate metrics and
   aggregate figures that cannot reconstruct individual raw measurements.
4. Citation of the experiment paper and explicit attribution to
   batteryarchive.org in every public output.

We will not redistribute the raw files, publish individual-cell measurement
tables, or claim commercial-use rights. If any of the uses above require a
separate permission or different wording, please let us know and we will follow
that boundary. We would also be happy to send you the final competition or
publication reference, as requested.

A short reply confirming the permitted scope would be very helpful.

Best regards,

Jincheng Liu
